# Clean Truck Check for Construction and Concrete Fleets in Northern California

A mixer, boom truck, or dump sitting on a DMV hold is not a paperwork problem. It is a missed pour. Clean Truck Check (CARB HD I/M) applies to nearly all diesel and alternative-fuel vehicles over 14,000 lb GVWR that run California public roads — including out-of-state plates that work NorCal jobs.

NorCal CARB Mobile tests at the yard or jobsite. OBD $75. OVI (J1667 opacity) $199. Motorhome $99 / $229. Call 916-890-4427.

This replaces the July 2025 construction/tree post that listed counties and no current prices. Prices, cadence, and corridor pages below are current as of 10 September 2026.

## What the program actually requires

- **Now through September 2027:** most heavy-duty diesels = **two tests per year** (≈17 weeks / 4 months from the last passing test). Agricultural vehicles that meet the HD I/M definition and California recreational / emergency motorhomes = one test per year.
- **Beginning October 2027:** OBD-equipped vehicles move to **four tests per year**. Opacity-path units (typically 2012-and-older diesel) stay twice a year.
- Source of truth is the fleet’s **CTC-VIS** record, not a wall calendar. A passing test can be submitted up to 90 days before the deadline.
- A fine is a bill. A **CA DMV registration hold** is a parked asset that still costs insurance and yard space.

Do not clear codes or pull batteries before an OBD scan. “Not Ready” burns the window.

Official program background: [CARB Clean Truck Check testing requirements](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-emissions-compliance-testing-requirements).

## The 17-week calendar

After a passing test, put the next window on the calendar at **+17 weeks** (about 4 months). That is the operating cadence for twice-yearly units. Do not wait for the Friday before a DMV hold.

A 10-truck construction fleet that drives each unit to a station twice a year burns driver hours in travel and queue. On-site, each truck is down about 15 minutes. After October 2027 the OBD half of that roster goes to four windows a year. The station model doubles again. The yard model does not.

See also [2026 testing deadlines](https://www.norcalcarbmobile.com/blog/2026-carb-testing-deadlines) and [pricing](https://www.norcalcarbmobile.com/pricing).

## What to have ready

1. Location where the trucks will be parked (yard or jobsite with access).
2. Count by test type if you know it — OBD vs OVI. If you do not know, we confirm on the unit.
3. CTC-VIS login. CARB annual fee is paid in the portal, separate from the test.
4. Keys in the truck. Engines off until we ask.

We submit passing results to CTC-VIS. If it is not uploaded, it did not happen.
