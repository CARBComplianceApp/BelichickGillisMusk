# Harvest Season Clean Truck Check — Ag Can Stay Annual, Haul Cannot

September is when Central Valley yards mix two clocks on the same pad. A truck used exclusively for agricultural operations, as CARB defines that term, stays on an **annual** Clean Truck Check. A dump, transfer, or interstate haul that is not exclusive-ag is on the **semi-annual** clock — about 17 weeks / 4 months from the last passing test.

NorCal CARB Mobile tests at the farm or the yard. OBD $75. OVI (J1667 opacity) $199. Call 916-890-4427.

Do not guess the class from the paint. Confirm in CTC-VIS.

## Two clocks, one harvest

| Class | Typical use | Test cadence | Calendar rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agricultural vehicle (HD I/M definition) | Farm product to first point of processing, exclusive-ag | **1× / year** | +300 days |
| CA recreational / emergency motorhome | Not commercial haul | **1× / year** | +300 days |
| Everything else over 14,000 lb GVWR on CA roads | Construction haul, port dray, general freight | **2× / year** through Sep 2027 | **+17 weeks** |
| OBD-equipped, beginning Oct 2027 | Same haul class | **4× / year** | Dispose the 2× series only when that VIN is actually on 4× |

Source of truth is the VIN in **CTC-VIS**, not the word “ag” on the door. A passing test may be submitted up to 90 days before the deadline.

Official requirements: [CARB emissions compliance testing](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-emissions-compliance-testing-requirements).

## Why harvest yards miss the window

The fee paid in CTC-VIS is not the test. A DMV hold parks the asset during the week you cannot spare it. “Not Ready” after a battery pull or code clear burns the 90-day window. Out-of-state plates working California roads stay in the state program.

Book the yard visit at the start of the window, not the Friday before a hold.

## What to have ready

1. Yard or field location with truck access.
2. Count by path if known — OBD (2013+ diesel / OBD-equipped) vs OVI (typically 2012-and-older).
3. CTC-VIS login, or $35 portal setup. CARB annual fee is paid in the portal, separate from the test.
4. Keys in the truck. Do not clear codes before we scan.

If it is not uploaded to CTC-VIS, it did not happen.
